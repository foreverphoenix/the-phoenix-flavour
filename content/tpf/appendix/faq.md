## Technical / Installation

**Can I delete the downloaded archives after the installation?**

Once Wabbajack has successfully installed TPF, the mod archives in the download folder are technically no longer needed. You could delete them to save space. However, if you want to update in the list in the future, all archives would have to be redownloaded. If possible, it is thus advisable to keep the downloaded archives.
