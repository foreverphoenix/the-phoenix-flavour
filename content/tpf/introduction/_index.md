## Overview

**The Phoenix Flavour** (TPF) is a mod list for Skyrim Special Edition. Originally published in 2017 as a manual modding guide, TPF is now exclusively available as a Wabbajack list for fast and easy installation. The list features around **2.000** carefully selected mods as well as numerous custom edits, bringing together much of the modding community's best work to date. Rediscover Skyrim as you know and love it – with modernised graphics, improved gameplay, and a plethora of new content!

### Features

See the [Load Order Library](https://loadorderlibrary.com/lists/the-phoenix-flavour) page for the full modlist or check the [Gameplay Guide](/tpf/gameplay/overview/) for detailed documentation.

- **A stable foundation** comprising all essential mods and fixes.
- **Curated Creation Club integration** retaining the best content from the Anniversary Edition DLC and removing the rest.
- **Consistent and performant visuals** featuring the [Community Shaders](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/86492) suite and hundreds of hand-picked asset replacers as well as custom LODs and facegen.
- **SimonRim-based gameplay** with reworks for all major systems, removing exploits and moderately increasing difficulty without fundamentally changing Skyrim's core gameplay loop.
- **High-quality content additions** introducing new lands, quests, followers, weapons, armors, spells, music, and more!
- **Moderate customisation** in the form of an optional interface overhaul ([Untarnished UI](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/75188)), additional mechanics tied to Survival Mode, and various minor tweaks.
- **Many custom modifications** including my tweaks and fixes for various mods, dozens of custom patches, and an exclusive balancing overhaul to ensure seamless integration across the board.

TPF does not include: Sexualised content, AI-generated content, 'hardcore' survival mechanics, Soulslike combat, or [Legacy of the Dragonborn](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/11802).
